## Part 4 - The three traps

Tick any that apply to your choice, and say why you are proceeding anyway.

- [ ] **Too clean.** A pre-scored competition file with its cleaning problems already
      solved by someone else, leaving you nothing to demonstrate.
- [ ] **Too big.** So many rows or columns that loading and wrangling eats the time
      Module 9 needs for presentation preparation.
- [ ] **No decision attached.** Interesting data, but you cannot say what decision it
      informs. This is the most common failure.
